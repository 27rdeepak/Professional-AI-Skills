# Phase 5 Examples

## Enterprise IT

Request: `Use $cloud-governance to assess our AWS account sprawl and exception process.`

Expected result:
- Clear summary of current governance gaps
- Distinction between policy design and enforcement
- Prioritized remediation actions

## Microsoft 365

Request: `Use $entra-id-governance to review privileged access and guest lifecycle controls.`

Expected result:
- Identity lifecycle risks called out explicitly
- Ownership and exception handling identified
- Practical next controls recommended

## Cybersecurity

Request: `Use $vulnerability-management to prioritize the backlog for this quarter.`

Expected result:
- Risk-based prioritization, not raw severity ordering
- Business impact and exposure context preserved
- Clear treatment plan with owner and timing

## Business

Request: `Use $business-case-building to compare a phased rollout with a big-bang launch.`

Expected result:
- Option comparison with costs, risks, and assumptions
- Recommendation tied to evidence
- Explicit trade-offs and decision criteria

## Leadership

Request: `Use $difficult-conversations to prepare for a performance reset discussion.`

Expected result:
- A conversation plan with purpose, facts, and boundaries
- Tone calibrated for directness and respect
- Concrete next-step contract
