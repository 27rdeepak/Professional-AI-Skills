# Code of Conduct

We pledge respectful, inclusive participation free from harassment. Engage with ideas and evidence, give specific feedback, respect privacy and attribution, disclose conflicts, and avoid deceptive or unsafe professional content.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Report incidents privately to maintainers. They may remove content or restrict participation based on severity and pattern while handling reports as confidentially as practical.
